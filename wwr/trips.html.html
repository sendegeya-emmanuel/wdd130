Available River Packages

The Ruvubu Rapids Challenge

Designed for adrenaline seekers! Experience 4 hours of Class IV rapids with steep drops and fast currents. Perfect for advanced rafters looking for teamwork challenges.

Tanganyika Family Scenic Float

A relaxing 2-hour gentle drift suitable for all ages, including children and grandparents. Enjoy beautiful views of the green mountains and local Burundi birdlife.

The Wilderness Exploration Track

An immersive full-day trip that combines Class II and III rapids with a scenic lunch break on a secluded river beach. All premium safety gear and meals are included.
